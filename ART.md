# 천장 청소반 — art direction

A night-shift maintenance card made playable. Warm paper (`#F7F3E9`) holds two ink-blue structural rails (`#203047`); teal (`#55B9AD`) denotes player/action, apricot (`#F4A65F`) collectible dust, coral (`#E87E82`) hazards, and muted periwinkle (`#8195CA`) secondary type. There is no borrowed sprite art: the cleaner, mop, dust, triangular hazards, diagonal rail marks, panels, progress meter, and paper flecks are drawn with Godot primitives in `game.gd`. The cleaner rotates with gravity; the vertical transfer and pickup/hit pulses provide motion. Controls use raised, outlined blocks with large touch targets, while desktop keeps the same visual grammar without the touch row. Layout uses an aspect-aware logical canvas: 700 logical units tall on narrow portrait and 640 on wider screens, so circles and typography do not stretch.

Primary typography: bundled Gowun Dodum Regular (`assets/GowunDodum-Regular.ttf`), licensed SIL OFL 1.1; copyright/license notice is `assets/OFL.txt` and explicitly included in the Web package. Header and instructions use Korean, with a restrained English shift label. Self-generated monophonic WAV cues distinguish gravity flip, dust pickup, damage and victory (`generate_audio.py`); no external audio or imagery.

Screens: title establishes upper/lower rails and the worker; tutorial is a five-line shift card; play overlays a compact three-bay meter, dust/hit counters, responsive controls and world; success/failure cards resolve the shift and restart. Desktop and 320×568 Chromium captures are under `screenshots/`.
